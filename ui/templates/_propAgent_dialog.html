📖 Step 4 — Basis & Journal Guide
🏗 Basis Frame

Gross Acquisition Cost = all Capitalize+Debit items summed (purchase price, title, recording, taxes, surveys…).

The assessor's land % splits the gross into Acct.Fixed.Land (non-depreciable) and Acct.Fixed.Tangible.InService (depreciable over 27.5 yrs for residential). These are the two fixed-asset entries posted to the GL.

📋 Journal Frame

Shows the exact records written to llcAssets. Every row clears through Acct.Cash.Escrow as the Ledger (counter-account), so the escrow account nets to $0 when the journal is balanced — no residual liability.

Fixed asset rows are highlighted green. Credit rows (Cash to Close, New Loan, Owner Capital) show the funding source that cancels the escrow balance.

Balance Sheet effect after commit:  Acct.Cash.Escrow = $0 (cleared) · Acct.Fixed.Land ↑ land amount · Acct.Fixed.Tangible.InService ↑ building amount · funding accounts (Bank / Mortgage / Capital) ↓ by their credit amounts.
