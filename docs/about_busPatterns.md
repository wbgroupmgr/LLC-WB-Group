# WBGroup - Business Patterns

The `W&B Group, LLC` started as simple and basic business idea: manage a single property rental home, with the goal of making a profit and learning the "residential rental business".   The key KPI being 

1. consistent, steady and high quality rental customers drive a consistent cash flow,
2. good maintanence and prudent improvements drive a high appreciation rate on owned properties (YTY min 8%),
3. ensure a steady ER base per property,  manage expense costs,
4. focus on long term, geneational wealth strategy (50-200 years).

Key to this was development of LLC Mgmt Services that focused on managing the financial accounting `books` with a well defined Standard Operating Procedures (see per business pattern.  

## Business Patterns

The LLC Management services needs to be tailored to each bussiness pattern with its own AI MCP (Model Context Protocol). The MCP is an open-source, universal communication standard that allows AI models (like Claude or ChatGPT) to securely connect to external tools, databases, and file systems -- ie. expert accounting/CPA/IRS knowledge.

### 1. Service Provider (Consulting, Agencies)

* Focus: Time tracking and billing utilization.
* Core Accounting:
   * High volume of accounts receivable
   * Minimal inventory
   * Heavy emphasis on payroll or contractor costs

### 2. Real Estate Rental (Residential/Commercial)

* Focus: Fixed asset tracking and passive activity.
* Core Accounting:
   * Heavy depreciation schedules
   * Separate tracking per property
   * Security deposit escrow liabilities

### 3. Retail & E-Commerce

* Focus: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) and inventory valuation.
* Core Accounting:
   * High-volume sales platform integrations
   * Sales tax liabilities across multiple jurisdictions
   * Shrink tracking

### 4. Construction & Contractors

* Focus: Job costing and project profitability.
* Core Accounting:
   * Percentage-of-completion revenue recognition
   * Progress billings (retention)
   * Tracking direct labor/materials per project

### 5. Manufacturing & Assembly

* Focus: Multi-stage inventory and overhead allocation.
* Core Accounting:
   * Raw materials, work-in-progress (WIP), and finished goods tracking
   * Standard costing variances

### 6. Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) / Subscription

* Focus: Deferred revenue and recurring billing cycles.
* Core Accounting:
   * ASC 606 revenue recognition compliance
   * Tracking customer acquisition cost (CAC)
   * Churn metrics

### 7. Hospitality & Food Service (Restaurants, Hotels)

* Focus: Daily cash reconciliation and high-turnover inventory.
* Core Accounting:
   * POS system matching
   * Tip credit tracking
   * Daily cash drawer audits
   * Perishable food COGS

### 8. Healthcare & Medical Practices

* Focus: Complex third-party billing and insurance collections.
* Core Accounting:
   * Tracking insurance vs. patient responsibility
   * High accounts receivable aging
   * HIPAA-compliant expense tracking

### 9. Digital Asset / Crypto Businesses

* Focus: Fair value asset tracking and transaction volume.
* Core Accounting:
   * High-frequency capital gains/losses tracking
   * Custodial wallet reconciliations
   * Fast-moving cost-basis accounting

### 10. Franchisees

* Focus: Royalty compliance and standardized reporting.
* Core Accounting:
   * Monthly gross revenue calculations for royalty fees
   * Marketing fund contributions
   * Rigid parent-company chart of accounts
